Our Heritage & Craft

Coffee. Community. Warm Coastal Culture.

Café Obregado is born from a passion for thoughtful culinary craft and unhurried hospitality.

Tucked away next to Platinum Heritage in Thane West, we invite you to pause the city rush. Every cup is brewed with scientific precision using top-tier Arabica beans ethically sourced from India's finest estates, while our kitchen honors authentic Goan culinary heritage with freshly baked poee bread prepared daily.

Specialty Brews

Ethically sourced single-origin Arabica beans, roasted to highlight rich cocoa notes and bright aromatics.

House-Made Poee

Traditional Goan fermented bread baked with authentic pockets, stuffed with savory, slow-simmered fillings.

Curated Culinary Selection

Our Signature Menu
